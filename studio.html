1. Sthana Bala (Positional)

Derived from sign dignity, exaltation distance, angles (Kendras), and 7 divisional charts. Governs worldly reputation, status, and stability of results.

2. Dig Bala (Directional)

Peak directional pull: Jupiter/Mercury in 1st (East), Sun/Mars in 10th (South), Saturn in 7th (West), Moon/Venus in 4th (North). Confers clear purpose, leadership, and public influence.

3. Kala Bala (Temporal)

Strength gained from day vs night birth (Natonnatha), bright vs dark fortnight (Paksha), and hourly planetary rulers. Determines auspicious timing and rhythm of life events.

4. Chesta Bala (Motional)

Reflects orbital velocity and retrogression. Retrograde Grahas receive full 60 Virupas because their closeness to Earth imparts extraordinary persistence and internal conviction.

5. Naisargika Bala (Natural)

Inherent cosmic luminosity. Sun is naturally most powerful (60 Virupas), followed by Moon, Venus, Jupiter, Mercury, Mars, and Saturn (8.57 Virupas).

6. Drik Bala (Aspectual)

Net aspectual influence from other Grahas. Aspects from benefics (Guru, Shukra, Budha) elevate performance; harsh malefic gaze adds friction and tests endurance.

Classical Transit & Ashtakavarga Evaluation Methodology

How Transit Quality is calculated per Phaladeepika (Ch. 26), Brihat Parasara Hora Shastra, & Sarvashtakavarga Paddhati

Classical Standard
🌙 1. Janma Rashi (Natal Moon) Rule

In classical Vedic astrology, transits are evaluated primarily from the Natal Moon (Janma Rashi) because the Moon represents the Mind (Manas) and directly experiences joy and grief in the physical realm.

🪐 Saturn & 🔴 Mars: Auspicious ONLY in 3 houses (3, 6, 11) from Moon (Upachayas). In all other 9 houses, their malefic fire causes friction, delays, Sade Sati (12, 1, 2), Kantaka (4), or Ashtama (8).
💖 Venus (Shukra): Auspicious in 9 houses (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 11, 12) from Moon! Unfavorable in only 3 houses (6, 7, 10). Bestows pleasure, harmony, and material ease.
♃ Jupiter (Guru): Auspicious in 5 houses (2, 5, 7, 9, 11) from Moon (Guru Bala). Brings divine grace, wisdom, expansion, and marriage/children blessings.
☀️ Sun: Auspicious in 3, 6, 10, 11 from Moon.
☿ Mercury: Auspicious in 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11 from Moon.
🌙 Moon: Auspicious in 1, 3, 6, 7, 10, 11 from Moon.
☊ ☋ Rahu & Ketu: Auspicious in 3, 6, 11 from Moon (Upachayas).
🛡️ 2. Sarvashtakavarga (SAV) Shield

While the house placement shows the intrinsic flavor of the transit, the SAV Bindus in that transited rashi dictate concrete material capacity & astral protection.

Standard Baseline 28 Bindus
337 total SAV points distributed across 12 signs gives an exact mean of ~28 points. 28 is neutral equilibrium.
High Support (Kavach / Shield) ≥ 30 Bindus
Acts as an astral shock-absorber. Even if Saturn or Mars is in a difficult house (e.g. 8th or Sade Sati), 30+ bindus cushions the impact and protects the native. In a good house, it brings immense success.
Average Support 25 – 29 Bindus
Balanced manifestation. Results require standard disciplined effort with steady progress.
Low Support (Karmic Friction) < 25 Bindus
Depleted astral vitality. Even an intrinsically auspicious transit house cannot bear full fruit without bindus; difficult transits strike with full resistance.
