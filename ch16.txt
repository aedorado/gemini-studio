According to classical texts the planets, Moon, Sun, Jupiter, Mars, Mercury, Saturn, Venus and Rahu have been evolved respectively from Yoginis, Mangala, Pingala, Dhanya etc.
Yogini is a Nakshatra based Dasha. The Mahadasha of each yogini starts with itself and follows in a regular order as above.
